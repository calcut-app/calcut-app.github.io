The CalCut Monthly — Terms

Hit your two daily rules more than any other member this month and CalCut pays you A$1,000.

Version 1.0 · Last updated 10 October 2026 · Applies to every Competition Month from November 2026

In brief

The full Terms below are what count.

The prize
One prize of AUD $1,000 each calendar month, to the eligible entrant with the most points. An exact tie splits it equally. No draw, no luck.
How points work
Points are for hitting CalCut's two daily rules — your calorie target and your step goal — never for weight lost. Joined CalCut mid-month? You start with points for the days before you joined.
Who can enter
Open to CalCut subscribers aged 18 or over who live in Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Canada (except Quebec) or the United States (except the states listed in clause 3.1), and who have opted in inside the app.
What it costs
It costs nothing beyond your normal CalCut subscription. Free access CalCut has given you counts as a subscription while it is active.
Each month closes
At 12:00 UTC on the 2nd of the following month. Open the app before then so your steps are counted. The result is announced in the app on the 3rd and confirmed before the prize is paid.
Who runs it
Apple is not a sponsor. Instagram is not a sponsor. CalCut Pty Ltd runs the Monthly.

2. WHAT THIS IS

2.1The Monthly is a competition of skill and effort. Each calendar month, the entrant who scores the most points for hitting CalCut's two daily rules wins. The Monthly is not a lottery, sweepstake, raffle or game of chance. There are no odds: the number of entrants in a month does not affect how the winner is decided.

2.2If two or more entrants finish a month on exactly the same number of points, the prize for that month is divided equally between them. No tie-break, draw or judging is used.

2.3One prize of AUD $1,000 is paid each month, to one member (or shared equally between tied winners under clause 2.2). Entering does not entitle any other entrant to any payment.

2.4Points are awarded for hitting your daily calorie and step rules, not for losing weight. Your weight is never scored, compared or published in the Monthly. "Pays you to lose weight" describes what following the two rules does; the competition itself rewards hitting the rules.

4. THE COMPETITION MONTH AND THE CLOSE

4.1Each Competition Month is a calendar month. Days are counted in the local time set on your device, as the app records them. Your month runs from the first to the last day of the calendar month in your local time.

4.2Scoring closes at 12:00 UTC on the second day of the following month ("the Close"). For example, November 2026 closes at 12:00 UTC on 2 December 2026 — 11:00pm Melbourne time, 7:00am New York time, 12:00 noon London time on 2 December.

4.3Steps reach CalCut only when you open the app. Your phone counts your steps, and the app reads them when it is opened. To make sure every day of your month is scored, open CalCut after your last day of the month and before the Close. Step data that first reaches CalCut after the Close is not scored for that month, whatever date it relates to.

4.4Food must be logged in the app on or before the Close to be scored.

4.5Standings shown in the app during a month are provisional. They change as data arrives and are not final until CalCut has completed the review in section 10 after the Close.

4.6Points are scored from the first day of the Competition Month, or from the day you entered if you entered part-way through a month, whichever is later.

4.7Head start. If your CalCut account was created after the first day of a Competition Month, you start that month with a head start of 4 points for each day of the month before the day your account was created (your local time), added when you enter. Your head start is capped so that you start below the entrant in 5th place at the moment you enter. It is given once per person, only in the month your account was created, and leaving and re-entering does not change it. It is for people new to CalCut: if you have held a CalCut account or subscription before, under any email address, you don't receive one, and a head start given in breach of this clause is removed at the review in section 10.

7. YOUR TARGETS

7.1CalCut scores two daily rules: staying at or under your daily calorie target, and meeting or exceeding your daily step goal. For the Monthly, the targets you are scored against are locked as described in this section, so that no entrant can change the rules mid-month.

7.2Calorie target. Your Monthly calorie target is the daily target the app recommends for the pace you have chosen, calculated from your profile and your latest logged weight on or before the first day of the month (or the day you entered, under clause 4.6). It is locked for the whole month. A calorie target you have typed in yourself is not used; if your typed target is higher than the recommended one, you are scored against the recommended one.

7.3Step goal — manual. If you set your own step goal, your Monthly step goal is locked on the first day of the month (or the day you entered) at the higher of 6,000 steps and the goal you have set, taken after any adjustment the app itself makes on that day. Lowering your goal during the month does not lower your Monthly step goal.

7.4Step goal — automatic. If the app sets your step goal for you ("automatic"), you are scored each day against the step goal the app had set for you on that day. The app raises and lowers automatic goals itself and you cannot set them; no minimum applies to them.

7.5Maintain mode. If your account is set to maintain your weight rather than lose it on any day of a month, the calorie rule is not scored for you on that day. The step rule is scored as normal.

7.6The app may adjust your live targets during the month exactly as it does for every member. Those adjustments do not change the locked targets above, and do not affect your eligibility.

7.7If you believe your locked targets are wrong, contact support@calcut.com.au within 7 days of the first day of the month (or of your entry). Requests made later may be declined.

8. HOW POINTS ARE SCORED

8.1A day counts towards the Monthly only as the app records it. Each day, two rules are assessed against your locked targets:

(a)The step rule is met when the steps the app reads for that day are at or above your Monthly step goal (clause 7.3 or 7.4).

(b)The calorie rule is met when the calories you logged for that day are between 60% and 100% of your Monthly calorie target (both inclusive), from at least two separate food entries. A day with nothing logged, a single entry, or a total below 60% of your target does not meet the calorie rule for the Monthly, even if the app shows that day as under your target.

8.2The 60% floor and the two-entry minimum exist so that a prize cannot be won by logging a fraction of what was eaten. The app uses the same rule to show a Perfect Day for every user.

8.3A "Perfect Day" for the Monthly is a day on which both rules in clause 8.1 are met.

8.4Points per day:

Met both — a Perfect Day8

A Perfect Day that is your third or later in a row+1 on top of the above

8.5The +1 in clause 8.4 is a flat bonus for each Perfect Day that is the third or later in an unbroken run of Perfect Days; it does not grow with the length of the run. No points are awarded for weight lost, for steps beyond your goal, for eating further below your target, or for anything else, except a head start under clause 4.7. Over-walking and under-eating earn nothing.

8.6Your month score is the sum of your daily points for the days scored under clause 4.6, plus any head start under clause 4.7, as they stand at the Close.

8.7The app's record is the record. Scores are determined solely from data recorded by the CalCut app. No other evidence of diet or activity is considered. Correspondence is entered into only in relation to a clear technical error, reported to support@calcut.com.au within 7 days of the day in question and in any case before the Close.
