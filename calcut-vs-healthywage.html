CalCut vs HealthyWage.

HealthyWage's main product is a bet on your own weight loss: reach your goal and you win, miss it and you lose your deposit. CalCut is a subscription calorie tracker with a monthly prize: A$1,000 every month to whoever's the most consistent. Most points for hitting your two daily rules wins. Terms.

We make CalCut, so read this with that in mind. HealthyWage's details come from its own website, rules and App Store listing, checked 5 November 2026. They can change, so check before you pay. We haven't tested its app for this page. Sources.

The short answer.

Choose HealthyWage if you

  • Have a set weight goal and a long timeframe
  • Want a possible prize above A$1,000
  • Want a result that depends on your own goal, not other people's scores
  • Are comfortable that you could lose your deposit
  • Use Android

Choose CalCut if you

  • Want a daily calorie and step tracker
  • Would rather not have money riding on a weigh-in
  • Want a new competition every month, knowing only one member wins
  • Want daily habits scored, not kilos

Quick answers.

What's the biggest difference?
What's at risk, and who you're up against. On a HealthyWage bet, you can lose your deposit, and the result depends on your own goal. On CalCut, there's no deposit, but one member wins each month, so you can do everything right and still not win.
Is CalCut available in the US?
The app is, and the Monthly is open to subscribers aged 18+ in the US except Arizona, Colorado, Maryland, Nebraska, New Jersey, North Dakota, Tennessee and Vermont. The prize is paid in your local currency at the exchange rate on the day of payment.
Do I have to lose weight to win on CalCut?
No. Points are for hitting your daily calorie and step goals. Weight lost never scores.
