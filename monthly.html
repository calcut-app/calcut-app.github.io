FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I have to lose the most weight to win?
No. Points are for hitting your daily calorie and step goals. Weight lost never scores, so your size makes no difference.
Can I still win if I join mid-month?
Yes. If you're new to CalCut and join after the 1st, you start that month with 4 points for each day before you joined, added when you enter and capped so you start just below 5th place. From there, hitting your goals does the climbing.
Is it a lucky draw?
No luck involved. The member with the most points at the close wins. If two or more finish exactly level, they split the prize equally.
Does it cost anything extra?
No entry fee. You need an active CalCut subscription — monthly or annual, including the annual plan's 7-day free trial.
How does the winner get paid?
We email the winner. Reply within 14 days and we pay within 28 days of a verified claim, by bank transfer, PayPal or Wise. Outside Australia it's converted to your currency on the day we pay, and we cover the fees.
When does a month close?
At 12:00 UTC on the 2nd of the following month. Open CalCut before then so your last steps are counted.
How do you keep it fair?
Before any prize is paid we review the top scores — food entries, calorie totals and step patterns. Honest logging is the only way to win.
Who sees my name?
Only the display name you choose and your points, on the Leaderboard and at the reveal. Never your real name, weight or email.
