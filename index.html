How much does CalCut cost?
CalCut is $15.99 AUD per month or $69.99 AUD per year (saves you ~64%). Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial — try the whole app risk-free before you're charged.
Do I need to count macros?
No. CalCut only cares about your daily calorie total — not the breakdown of protein, carbs, and fat. Macros are tracked in the background if you want to see them, but they're never the goal.
How do I log food?
Three ways. Scan a barcode. Take a photo of your meal and let the AI estimate it. Or just describe what you ate ("two slices of toast with peanut butter") and it figures out the rest. Pick whichever's fastest.
What if I miss a day?
Nothing happens. No streak shaming, no guilt-trip notifications. Tomorrow's a new day. CalCut is built around consistency over weeks, not punishment for slip-ups.
Does CalCut work with Apple Health?
Yes. CalCut reads your daily step count from Apple HealthKit automatically. No fitness tracker required — your iPhone is enough.
Is my data private?
Yes. We don't sell your data, ever. Your weight, photos, and food logs are stored encrypted and only visible to you. Read the full privacy policy.
