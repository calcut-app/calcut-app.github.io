CalCut vs Easy Diet Diary.

Both are Australian calorie trackers. Easy Diet Diary is free and built for detailed food tracking. CalCut is a paid subscription built around two daily rules and a monthly prize: A$1,000 every month to whoever's the most consistent. Most points for hitting your two daily rules wins. Terms.

We make CalCut, so read this with that in mind. Easy Diet Diary's details come from its own website and App Store listing, checked 5 November 2026. They can change. We haven't tested its app for this page. Sources.

The short answer.

Choose Easy Diet Diary if you

  • Want it free, with no ads
  • Want a dedicated Australian food database
  • Track kilojoules, macros or micronutrients
  • Share your diary with a dietitian
  • Use Android

Choose CalCut if you

  • Don't want to count macros
  • Would rather photograph or describe a meal
  • Want your steps counted automatically
  • Want a monthly competition as a reason to log, knowing only one member wins

Quick answers.

Is Easy Diet Diary really free?
Its website and App Store listing say it's free and ad-free, with no hidden costs. CalCut is a paid subscription, so if cost decides it, Easy Diet Diary wins.
Which is better for Australian foods?
If you want a dedicated Australian database, choose Easy Diet Diary. CalCut can look up a barcode, estimate from a photo or work from your description, and it doesn't compete on database size.
Do I have to lose weight to win on CalCut?
No. Points are for hitting your daily calorie and step goals. Weight lost never scores.
