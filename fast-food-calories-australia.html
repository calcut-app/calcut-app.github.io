Quick answers.

What's the lowest-calorie fast food in Australia?

It depends on what you're counting as a meal. On the figures here, a single taco or slice sits under 200 Calories, and the lighter burgers (Whopper Jnr, Hamburger) are just under 300.

Does lower calorie mean healthier?

Not by itself. These pages only compare Calories. A lighter order can still be high in salt or leave you hungry an hour later.

How accurate are the figures?

They come from each chain's own published data, on the date listed on each page. Chains change recipes and sizes, so treat them as a guide and check the chain for the latest.
