4.4Entries completed outside the Entry Period will not be accepted, and CalCut is not obliged to notify you that you have not entered.

6. THE TWO RULES AND YOUR TARGETS

6.1The Challenge is scored on two daily rules: staying at or under your personalised daily calorie target, and meeting or exceeding your personalised daily step goal.

6.2A "Perfect Day" means a day on which both rules were met, as recorded by the CalCut app.

6.3Your calorie and step targets are set by CalCut and are locked for the duration of the Challenge. You may not set or alter your step goal yourself, whether during onboarding or at any point during the Challenge Period.

6.4CalCut may adjust your targets at any time. Where you have automatic step tracking enabled in settings, your step goal will be updated by the app as appropriate. Adjustments made by CalCut or by the app are permitted and expected, and do not affect your eligibility.

6.5If you believe your targets have been set incorrectly, contact us at support@calcut.com.au during the Entry Period and we will review them. Requests made after the Entry Period may be declined.

6.6Any attempt to manually alter, override or circumvent your calorie or step targets will result in disqualification.

9. HOW THE MAIN PRIZES ARE DECIDED

9.1 Most Perfect Days

(a)Awarded to the entrant with the highest number of Perfect Days recorded in the app across the Challenge Period.

(b)In the event of a tie, the prize is awarded to the tied entrant with the greatest cumulative number of steps recorded above their step goal across the Challenge Period.

(c)If a tie remains, CalCut will award the prize at its discretion.

(d)Progress photographs are not required for this prize.

9.2 Most Steps Total

(a)Awarded to the entrant with the highest cumulative step count recorded in the app across the Challenge Period.

(b)Progress photographs are not required for this prize.

9.3 Best Transformation

(a)To be considered, an entrant must have recorded a minimum of twenty (20) Perfect Days out of 56 and must have submitted both before and after photographs in accordance with section 10.

(b)CalCut will shortlist between three (3) and five (5) finalists from eligible entrants, assessed on visible physical change and consistency record.

(c)If fewer than three (3) entrants satisfy clause 9.3(a), CalCut may shortlist from among all entrants who submitted both before and after photographs in accordance with section 10, and may shortlist fewer than three finalists.

(d)The winner will be selected by public vote conducted via a poll on the @calcut.app Instagram account. The finalist receiving the highest number of votes wins.

(e)Finalists will be asked for their express consent before any photograph is published. A finalist who declines will be replaced by the next-ranked eligible entrant.

(f)The public vote result is final. CalCut reserves the right to void votes it reasonably believes to have been obtained by automated means, purchased, or otherwise manipulated.

9.4 All determinations under this section are made using data recorded by the CalCut app. CalCut's records are the sole source of truth. No correspondence will be entered into regarding scoring, other than in relation to a clear technical error.
