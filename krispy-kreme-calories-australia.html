Quick answers.

How many calories are in a Krispy Kreme Original Glazed?

183 Calories, or about 6,400 steps to walk off.

How many calories are in a dozen Krispy Kreme doughnuts?

A dozen Original Glazed comes to 2,196 Calories, or 9,168 kJ. Most other flavours here are heavier.

Which Krispy Kreme has the fewest calories?

Of the doughnuts here, the Traditional Cinnamon at 160.
