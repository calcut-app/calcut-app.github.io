Calories in beer, wine and spirits.

A full-strength stubby is 136 Calories, a schooner is 154 and a pint is 207. A 150 mL glass of wine is about 104 to 120. Here are the figures in Australian serves, with the steps it would take to walk each one off.

Quick answers.

How many calories are in a schooner of beer?

154 Calories for a 425 mL schooner of full-strength beer, or about 5,400 steps to walk off.

How many calories are in a stubby?

A 375 mL stubby is about 90 for light beer, 115 for mid-strength and 136 for full-strength.

How many calories are in a glass of wine?

A 150 mL glass is about 106 for white, 110 for sparkling, 104 for rosé and 120 for red.

Which drink has the fewest calories?

Of the drinks here, a 30 mL nip with soda or a diet mixer is about 64. In a gin and tonic, regular tonic adds about 51 Calories to the 64.
