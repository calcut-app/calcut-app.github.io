Quick answers.

How many calories are in a GYG burrito?

769 Calories, mild with grilled chicken, or about 26,900 steps to walk off. With ground beef it's 824.

How many calories are in a GYG burrito bowl?

655 Calories, mild with grilled chicken. That's 114 fewer than the burrito.

What's low calorie at Guzman y Gomez?

The $3 Taco with beef is 161, a Hard Shell Taco is 186 and a Soft Taco is 192. Three Soft Tacos come to 576, which is less than one bowl.

Are GYG's sides heavier than the mains?

Some are. Large Queso Fries are 736 and Nacho Fries are 1,010, against 769 for the burrito.
