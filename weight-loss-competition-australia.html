Weight loss competitions in Australia.

Most of what's out there is a bet: you put money on a goal and can lose it. Here's what's open to Australians, and what to check before you enter. We make CalCut, so read this with that in mind. Details of other apps come from their own App Store listings and websites, checked 6 November 2026. They can change, so check before you pay.

What you can enter.

Bets on your own goal

  • HealthyWage: has an Australian site. You choose a wager and a goal of at least 10% of your weight, over 6 to 18 months. Miss it and you lose your deposit.
  • DietBet: you pledge money to join a game. Its listing says winners get their pledge back, plus more.

The CalCut Monthly

  • A$1,000 every month to whoever's the most consistent. Most points for hitting your two daily rules wins.
  • Weight lost never scores, so it isn't a weigh-off
  • No entry fee, but you need an active CalCut subscription
  • One winner each month, and exact ties split the prize

What to check before you enter.

What decides the winner? Weight lost, a percentage of your weight, or something else. A goal you could only hit by crash dieting isn't worth the prize.

What can you lose? Some competitions keep your money if you miss your goal. Others charge a fee or subscription whether you win or not.

How many people get paid? Some pay everyone who hits a goal. Some split a pot. Some, like the CalCut Monthly, pay one winner.

How is it checked? Look for how results are verified, and what you'd have to show to claim a prize.

Who runs it? Look for a named company, full terms and a date for when prizes are paid. Local gyms and councils also run challenges, so ask them for their terms too.

Quick answers.

Is there a weight loss competition in Australia in 2026?

Yes, though the options differ. HealthyWage has an Australian site, and the CalCut Monthly runs every calendar month and is open to Australian subscribers aged 18 or over. Local gyms and councils also run their own.

Can you win money for losing weight in Australia?

Some programs pay cash for reaching a weight goal, usually after you've put your own money in. The CalCut Monthly pays A$1,000 for consistency rather than kilos, so weight lost doesn't count.

Is the CalCut Monthly a lucky draw?

No. The member with the most points at the close wins. There's no draw, and an exact tie splits the prize.
