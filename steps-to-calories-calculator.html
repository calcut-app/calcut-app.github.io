Quick answers.

How many steps does it take to burn 500 calories?

About 17,500 steps for an 80 kg adult, or 12.5 km. At 60 kg it's about 23,300, and at 100 kg about 14,000.

How many steps does it take to burn 1,000 calories?

About 35,000 steps at 80 kg, or 25 km.

How many steps does it take to burn 100, 200 or 300 calories?

At 80 kg, about 3,500, 7,000 and 10,500 steps.

How many steps burn 1 kg of fat?

Using the rule of thumb of about 7,000 Calories per kg, about 245,000 steps at 80 kg, or 175 km. Real bodies don't follow the rule exactly.

How many steps a day do I need to lose weight?

There's no single number, because it depends on what you eat. To lose about 0.5 kg a week from walking alone, an 80 kg adult would need about 17,500 extra steps every day. That's why most people combine a daily step goal with a calorie target.

Why is this higher than other calculators?

Many count everything you burn while walking, including the energy you'd burn sitting still, which comes to about 24 steps per Calorie at 80 kg. We count only the extra energy walking burns, about 35 per Calorie, because that's what walking something off actually means.

How we worked it out

The American College of Sports Medicine's walking equation gives 0.1 mL of oxygen per kg per metre above resting. At about 5 Calories per litre of oxygen, that's 0.5 Calories per kg per km. With about 1,400 steps per km, steps per Calorie is 2,800 divided by your weight in kg. It's valid at normal walking speeds on flat ground, and your stride and pace will change the answer. Time assumes about 100 steps a minute, a commonly used threshold for moderate walking (Tudor-Locke et al., 2019).
