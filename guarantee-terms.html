8. THE CONDITION: 50 GREEN DAYS OUT OF 60

8.1CalCut scores two daily rules: staying at or under your daily calorie target, and meeting or exceeding your daily step goal.

8.2A "Green Day" is a day on which the app records both rules as met.

8.3To qualify for payment you must record at least 50 Green Days within your 60-day Guarantee Period. Up to 10 days may be missed for any reason. The days need not be consecutive.

8.4A day is scored as the app records it at the end of that day in your device's local time. Food must be logged in the app for the calorie rule to be assessed; a day with no food logged is not a Green Day. Steps are read automatically from Apple Health.

8.5The app's record is the record. Determinations under this section are made solely from data recorded by the CalCut app. No other evidence of diet or activity is considered. Correspondence is entered into only in relation to a clear technical error, reported to support@calcut.com.au within 7 days of the day in question.

9.3Each photograph must show the whole face of the scale with the reading clearly legible, taken at the time the weight is logged. Use the same scale for both weigh-ins where possible. Weigh yourself under similar conditions each time, for example first thing in the morning, before eating, in light clothing. These are recommendations to protect your own result; they are not grounds for disqualification.

9.4The weight you log must be your actual weight as shown on the scale at that time. A logged weight that does not match its photograph, or a starting weight that is inconsistent with weights logged before or after it, may be treated under section 11.

9.5Verification. 9.6Weigh-in photographs are stored privately, are used only to administer the Guarantee, are never published, and are handled under section 14.
