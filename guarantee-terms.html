8. THE CONDITION: 50 GREEN DAYS OUT OF 60

8.1CalCut scores two daily rules: staying at or under your daily calorie target, and meeting or exceeding your daily step goal.

8.2A "Green Day" is a day on which the app records both rules as met.

8.3To qualify for payment you must record at least 50 Green Days within your 60-day Guarantee Period. Up to 10 days may be missed for any reason. The days need not be consecutive.

8.4A day is scored as the app records it at the end of that day in your device's local time. Food must be logged in the app for the calorie rule to be assessed; a day with no food logged is not a Green Day. Steps are read automatically from Apple Health.

8.5The app's record is the record. Determinations under this section are made solely from data recorded by the CalCut app, read by CalCut when you claim. No other evidence of diet or activity is considered. Correspondence is entered into only in relation to a clear technical error, reported to support@calcut.com.au within 7 days of the day in question.

9.1Starting weigh-in. Your starting weight is the weight you enter in the app during setup on Day 1 under clause 5.1(b).

9.2Final weigh-in. You must log your final weight in the app, using its ordinary weight log, during the Claim Window: from the start of Day 60 to 11:59pm on Day 63 of your Guarantee Period. If no final weigh-in is recorded in the Claim Window, your entry ends and no payment is due. CalCut will email your pre-registered address before the Claim Window opens. Keeping track of your Day 60 is your responsibility.

9.3Photographs. At each of the two weigh-ins, take a photograph of your scale showing the reading you log, and keep it. The app does not collect these photographs. If you make a claim, email both photographs to support@calcut.com.au within 7 days of claiming. Each photograph should show the face of the scale with the reading clearly legible.

9.4Use the same scale for both weigh-ins where possible, and weigh yourself under similar conditions each time, for example first thing in the morning, before eating, in light clothing. These are recommendations to protect your own result; they are not grounds for disqualification.

9.5The weight you log must be your actual weight as shown on the scale at that time. A logged weight that does not match its photograph, or a starting weight that is inconsistent with weights logged before or after it, may be treated under section 11.

9.6Verification. Before paying a claim, CalCut may ask you to confirm your final weight by a short live video weigh-in arranged with CalCut support within 7 days of your claim, or to provide other reasonable evidence. A request for verification is not an accusation. Declining or not completing a reasonable verification request means the claim cannot be paid.

9.7Missing or unclear photographs. Where a photograph is missing or unclear, CalCut may accept other evidence, or may pay the claim without it where, having regard to your full logging record and any verification under clause 9.6, it is satisfied that the result is genuine. That decision is made in good faith and is at CalCut's discretion.

9.8Photographs you send are stored privately, used only to administer the Guarantee, never published, and handled under section 14.
