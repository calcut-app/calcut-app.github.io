Quick answers.

How many calories are in a Big Mac in Australia?

621 Calories, or about 21,700 steps to walk off. A Double Big Mac is 813.

How many calories are in a Big Mac meal?

With small fries (219) and a medium Coke (136), it's 976. With a medium Coke Zero (1) instead, it's 841.

What's low calorie at Maccas?

These are all under 350: small fries (219), 6 Chicken McNuggets (229), a Hamburger (294), a Cheeseburger (319) and a Filet-O-Fish (337). Low in calories doesn't mean filling, so check it suits your day.

What's the lowest-calorie McDonald's breakfast?

Of the McMuffins, the Bacon & Egg at 282. A Hash Brown is 144 on its own. Hotcakes with butter and syrup are 595.

Which McCafé coffee has the fewest calories?

A long black or espresso has 0. A piccolo latte has 39. A medium full cream latte has 160, and an Iced Velvet Mocha has 557.
