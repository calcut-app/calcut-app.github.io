Quick answers.

How many calories are in a slice of Domino's pizza?

Between 116 and 173 for a Classic Crust slice of the pizzas here, where a slice is one-eighth of a large.

How many calories are in a whole large Domino's pizza?

Eight Classic Crust slices come to 928 for a Margherita and 1,384 for a Mega Meatlovers.

Does thin crust save many calories?

A little. On these figures it saves about 11 to 19 Calories a slice. The toppings make a bigger difference than the base.

What's the lowest-calorie pizza at Domino's?

Of the pizzas here, the Margherita at 116 a slice, or 101 on Thin & Crispy.
