Why we can promise this

It isn't a diet secret. It's arithmetic.

Pick the 0.75 kg a week option and CalCut sets you a calorie number 750 below what your body burns, plus a step goal. Hold that for 60 days and it adds up to about 6 kg. The app does the sums and turns the day green when you hit both.

The app records every day, so both of us can see what happened. Follow the rules and it still doesn't happen? Then the maths was wrong, and we pay.

Straight answers

The questions we'd ask.

Is this a bet? Do I put money in?

No. You don't stake anything. The only cost is the CalCut annual plan, which is the same price with or without the guarantee, and if you cancel inside the free trial you pay nothing at all. If you meet the conditions and don't lose 6 kg, we pay you $600 out of our pocket.

What if I only lose 5.5 kg?

We pay. Six means six. If you've done your 50 green days and the scale says 5.9, that's $600.

What if I miss a day?

You get ten. Fifty green days out of sixty, in any order. A wedding, a sick day, a Friday that got away from you. Ten of those are fine.

What exactly counts as a green day?

You logged your food and finished the day at or under your calorie number, and your steps reached the goal. The app shows you both live and turns the day green when they're done. What the app records is what counts.

How do you know my weight is real?

You photograph your scale on day one and on day 60 and send us both if you claim. We may also ask for a quick live video weigh-in. If you're honest, that's a two-minute call. And if you did every green day and forgot the first photo, talk to us. We look at your whole record, not one picture.

How and when do you pay?

Bank transfer or PayPal, your choice, within 28 days of your claim being verified. We cover any fees.
