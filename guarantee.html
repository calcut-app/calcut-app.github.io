Why we can promise this

It isn't a diet secret. It's arithmetic.

CalCut gives you a calorie number 750 below what your body burns in a day, and a step goal. Hold that gap for 60 days and it adds up to about 6 kg. The app does the sums, sets the numbers, and turns the day green when you hit both.

The only way the weight stays is if the two rules don't get followed, and the app records every single day, so both of us can see exactly what happened. Follow them and it still doesn't happen? Then the maths was wrong, and we pay.

Straight answers

The questions we'd ask.

Is this a bet? Do I put money in?

No. You don't stake anything. The only cost is the CalCut annual plan, which is the same price with or without the guarantee. If you meet the conditions and don't lose 6 kg, we pay you $600 out of our pocket.

What if I only lose 5.5 kg?

We pay. Six means six. If you've done your 50 green days and the scale says 5.9, that's $600.

What if I miss a day?

You get ten. Fifty green days out of sixty, in any order. A wedding, a sick day, a Friday that got away from you. Ten of those are fine.

What exactly counts as a green day?

You logged your food and finished the day at or under your calorie number, and your steps reached the goal. The app shows you both live and turns the day green when they're done. What the app records is what counts.

How do you know my weight is real?

You photograph the scale through the app on day one and on day 60. Before we pay, we may ask for a quick live video weigh-in with our support team. If you're honest, that's a two-minute call.

How and when do you pay?

Bank transfer or PayPal, your choice, within 28 days of your day-60 weigh-in being verified. We cover any fees.

Can I stop?

Any time, from Settings in the app. Leaving ends the guarantee and unlocks your targets. It doesn't touch your subscription, which you manage through the App Store as usual.

Why do I register here first?

So the app knows to offer you the guarantee after you subscribe. It's only shown to people who registered here. Use the same email in the app, or the code we give you if you sign up a different way.

What if the app says I'm not eligible?

It means a 750-calorie gap would put you under a safe daily minimum, so the app won't set it. Nothing is recorded, your trial is still yours, and you can cancel it or keep CalCut on its normal settings.
