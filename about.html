About CalCut.

Who makes CalCut, and how we check the numbers on this site.

What CalCut is.

CalCut is a calorie and step tracker for iPhone, built around two daily rules: stay in your calorie target and hit your step goal. A$1,000 every month to whoever's the most consistent. Most points for hitting your two daily rules wins. Terms.

It's made by CalCut Pty Ltd (ACN 697 215 660) in Melbourne. CalCut isn't a medical service, and nothing on this site is medical advice.

How we check our numbers.

Food figures come from each chain's own published data or the Australian Food Composition Database. Each page says where the figures came from and when we read them.

Calculators show their formula and sources on the page. They're estimates, and they run in your browser, so nothing you type is saved or sent.

Comparisons with other apps use those apps' own listings and websites, with a date. We make CalCut, so we say so, and we say where another app is the better fit.

If something is wrong, email support@calcut.com.au and we'll check it and fix it.
