Quick answers.

Is there a free alternative to MyFitnessPal in Australia?

Yes. Easy Diet Diary's listing says it's completely free with no ads. CalCut isn't free: it's a paid subscription.

Is MyFitnessPal better than CalCut?

It does more, and its database is far larger. CalCut is simpler by design: two daily rules and a monthly prize. If you want detailed nutrition, MyFitnessPal is the better fit.

Does MyFitnessPal own Cal AI?

Yes. MyFitnessPal announced the deal on 2 March 2026, and said Cal AI would keep running as a separate app.
