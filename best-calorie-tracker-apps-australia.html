How to choose.

If you don't want to pay, start with Easy Diet Diary. It says it's free with no ads, and it's made in Australia with Australian food data.

If you want the biggest database, look at MyFitnessPal. It lists over 20.5 million foods, and its free and paid features differ, so check what you need before you start.

If logging is what makes you quit, pick an app built around fast logging, such as Cal AI or CalCut. Check what's behind a paywall first.

If you want more structure, look at Lose It! for a calorie budget and schedule, or Noom for daily lessons and optional coaching.

If you've tried diaries and stopped, that's who we built CalCut for. There are two daily rules: stay in your calorie target and hit your step goal. Quick answers.

What's the best free calorie counter in Australia?

For a fully free option, Easy Diet Diary is the obvious one to try: its listing says all features are free with no ads, and its food data is Australian. MyFitnessPal and Lose It! are free to download, with paid tiers.

Is there a calorie tracker that uses kilojoules?

Yes. Easy Diet Diary lets you track kilojoules or Calories. CalCut uses Calories.

Which app lets you log food with a photo?

Cal AI is built around it, and CalCut can estimate from a photo too. Cal AI's listing says scan results need a subscription, and CalCut is a paid subscription.
