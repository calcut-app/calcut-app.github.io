Cal AI alternatives.

Cal AI made photo logging popular, but it isn't the only way to track. Here's what else to look at, and how CalCut compares. We make CalCut, so read this with that in mind. Quick answers.

Is there a free alternative to Cal AI?

Easy Diet Diary is free, but it's built around searching and scanning barcodes rather than estimating from a photo. CalCut isn't free: it's a paid subscription too.

Is Cal AI worth it?

That depends on whether photo logging is what keeps you tracking. It has far more ratings than CalCut, at 18,112 against 32 in Australia. Its subscription options are listed on the App Store, so compare them before you start.

Who owns Cal AI?

MyFitnessPal announced on 2 March 2026 that it had bought Cal AI, and said it would keep running as a separate app.
