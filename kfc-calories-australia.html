Quick answers.

How many calories are in a Zinger Burger?

448 Calories, or about 15,700 steps to walk off. An Original Crispy Burger is also 448.

How many calories are in a piece of KFC chicken?

One piece is 235 Calories and three pieces are 705.

What's low calorie at KFC?

A regular Potato & Gravy (64), a regular Coleslaw (93), one piece of chicken (235), regular chips (283) and 6 nuggets (316) are all lighter than any burger here. Of the mains, the Zinger Crunch Bowl is 415.

How does a Mega Chicken Box compare with a day's food?

A regular Mega Chicken Box is 1,404 Calories. KFC's guide uses 8,700 kJ, about 2,079 Calories, as an average adult's day, so one box is about two-thirds of that.
